# Segmentation Settings

This dialog displays the settings for the segmenting the data during the analysis. Here are the options:

<table><thead><tr><th width="252.6666259765625" valign="top">Option</th><th valign="top">Description</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td valign="top">Last Analyzed</td><td valign="top">Displays the last analysis date/time.</td></tr><tr><td valign="top">Delimiters</td><td valign="top">Responses will be split based on these characters. For example, if the response contains a list of items separated by commas, you could put a comma in the Delimiters field and the responses are split into segments. This field may be left blank.</td></tr><tr><td valign="top">Characters To Ignore</td><td valign="top">These characters will be ignored in the responses. This field may be left blank.</td></tr><tr><td valign="top">Words To Ignore</td><td valign="top">These words will be ignored in the responses. Separate the words with a space. This field may be left blank.</td></tr><tr><td valign="top">Numerics Only</td><td valign="top">You can limit responses to groups of numerics only. This action is useful when auto-coding a question with both text and numeric responses, such as an Other Specify.</td></tr><tr><td valign="top">Minimum Frequency</td><td valign="top">This field controls the display of segments based on frequency. If a segment count does not equal or exceed this number, it does not display.</td></tr></tbody></table>
