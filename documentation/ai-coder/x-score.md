# X Score

X-Score™ stands for “Experience Score”. It’s a new satisfaction measure that shows overall satisfaction and key drivers of satisfaction. X-Score™ is the only measure that provides the “why’s” driving satisfaction, because it’s the only one based on people’s authentic (open end) comments about their experience and not based on a rating (closed end) scale. It has many applications, including Customer Experience Management, Patient Experience Management and Employee Feedback Management industries.

X-Score™ is similar to Net Promoter Score (NPS), using the same overall -100 to +100 rating scale, but is easier to implement given that it’s solely based on verbatim feedback.

X-Score™ shows overall satisfaction as well as the key drivers of satisfaction.

* X-Score™ is a new customer experience score that goes a step further than measures like NPS by providing the “whys” driving satisfaction.
* X-Score™ is based on experience data, people’s authentic, unfiltered verbatim comments, analyzed with Advanced Natural Language Processing (NLP) and calculated using a patent pending methodology.
* X-Score™ weighs the sentiment (perception/emotion) of the topics mentioned and their frequency in order to highlight the most common and impactful topics that drive satisfaction.
* X-Score™ is reported with numbers between -100 and +100. The closer to +100, the higher the satisfaction (similar to other common experience measure scores).
* X-Score™ can be compared across groups and waves so it’s possible to establish benchmarks and trends.
* X-Score™ is calculated in three steps (patent pending):
  * (1) Calculate the average sentiment value for a topic.
  * (2) Rescale the average to a value between -1 and +1.
  * (3) Multiply that average by the topic’s relative frequency versus the other topics.
