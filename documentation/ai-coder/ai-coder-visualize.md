# AI Coder Visualize

The visualization dialog allows you to see and understand your data with various charts and graphs. A set of pre-built dashboards, which focus on the most commonly used insights, are available. Or you can build your own custom dashboard by selecting the available chart options.

## Pre-Built Dashboards <a href="#minitocbookmark2" id="minitocbookmark2"></a>

Here are the options for the pre-built dashboards:

<table><thead><tr><th width="256" valign="top">Option</th><th valign="top">Description</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td valign="top">Overview</td><td valign="top">This option gives a general summary of insights or an executive overview featuring the frequency bar chart, co-occurrences, and cross-tabs using closed-ends.</td></tr><tr><td valign="top">Insights on Frequency</td><td valign="top">The insights report focusses on the most frequently mentioned codes or themes and how they are represented across the closed ends.</td></tr><tr><td valign="top">Sentiment Overview</td><td valign="top">The sentiment overview consists of sentiment bar chart by code, sentiment pie chart for overall sentiment, and sentiment cross-tab.</td></tr><tr><td valign="top">Closed Ends</td><td valign="top">Displays results from closed-ended (quantitative) questions, letting you see distribution and frequency</td></tr></tbody></table>

## Chart Options <a href="#minitocbookmark3" id="minitocbookmark3"></a>

Each chart has the option to download the chart (PNG image), download the data (CSV file).

Here are the common options for each chart:

<table><thead><tr><th width="269.333251953125" valign="top">Option</th><th valign="top">Description</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td valign="top">Top N Nets</td><td valign="top">Number of nets to display based on frequency</td></tr><tr><td valign="top">Top N Codes/Net</td><td valign="top">Number of codes to display in a net, based on frequency</td></tr><tr><td valign="top">Codes and Nets Checkboxes</td><td valign="top">Select to display codes and nets or just one of them.</td></tr><tr><td valign="top">Net Level</td><td valign="top">Allows you to display sub-nets</td></tr><tr><td valign="top">Value</td><td valign="top">Select from count, percentages, sentiment (counts), sentiment (percentages) or <a href="../cx-inspector-with-theme-extractor/x-score.md">X-Score</a>.</td></tr><tr><td valign="top">Banners</td><td valign="top">If closed-ends are available, you can select them as banners for cross-tabs.</td></tr><tr><td valign="top">Distribution Question</td><td valign="top">If you select a closed-end or sentiment, the charts are filtered by that question or sentiment.</td></tr><tr><td valign="top"><p>Display Related Comments</p><p> </p></td><td valign="top">A list of the comments related to the question displays on the right side of the screen.</td></tr></tbody></table>
