# Word Proximities

The Word Proximities report performs word counts around a specific word. If you also use a verbatim or responses visualization next to it, you can click a word in the proximities report and view the associated verbatim.

After you add a Word Proximities report to the canvas, the next step is to select a word or topic from the drop-down list in the Properties pane.&#x20;

You also have these options:
