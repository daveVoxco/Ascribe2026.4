# Hide and Unhide Topics

Users can hide and re-display topics in most visualizations. This action allows users to see how the visualization would look without that item.

To hide a topic in a cross-tab, code cloud, co-occurrence chart, and grid, use CTRL/ALT/Click on the topic.&#x20;

When working with charts, use CTRL/ALT/Click on part that represents the topic (ie, pie slice or bar), rather than the text label.

The chart title indicates a topic is hidden with (-1), followed by the hidden topic in brackets.&#x20;

To re-display the topic, click the Undo button.&#x20;

This option does not apply to correlation matrix, star plot, verbatim, or correspondence analysis. Word cloud has its own option of Ignore Word.
