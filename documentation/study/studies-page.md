## Study Status Filters

Study Status filters appear below the toolbar for quick access.

* Click a button to toggle a status on or off
* When off, the button displays white with a colored outline
* A brief delay after clicking allows multiple selections before refresh

Other columns have filters that display on hover over the column header.

Study status indicates the completion stage. Usage varies by department—some use all statuses, others use only Coding in Progress and Archived.

<table><thead><tr><th width="198.33331298828125" valign="top">Status</th><th valign="top">Description</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td valign="top">Under Construction</td><td valign="top">The study is not ready to code. Use this status while you prepare a study. For example, use this status if the data or the codebooks are not ready. The study will not appear on the Studies in Progress page or Studies to Transcribe page. (Coders and transcribers will not be able to access the study). Studies restored as "New" have a default status of "Under Construction."</td></tr><tr><td valign="top">In Progress</td><td valign="top">The study is ready to code or the coding has started. The study can be accessed from the Studies in Progress page or the Studies to Transcribe page (or any page).</td></tr><tr><td valign="top">On Hold</td><td valign="top">The study cannot be accessed from the Studies in Progress page, the Studies to Transcribe page or the Studies to Translate page. There may be a problem or a change to the study, and all work should be stopped until the problems are resolved.</td></tr><tr><td valign="top">Review in Progress</td><td valign="top">Use this status while the user does a quality check of the study. For example, use when the codes are checked for accuracy or low mentions. The study cannot be accessed from the Studies in Progress page, the Studies to Transcribe page, or the Studies to Translate page Also, you can use this status when you create or deliver study output.</td></tr><tr><td valign="top">Complete</td><td valign="top">The study is complete; data is ready to be downloaded for analysis.</td></tr><tr><td valign="top">Archived</td><td valign="top">The study is removed from all lists of active studies. If study shipping is enabled, all archived studies are sent to your FTP site on the Saturday after the status is changed to archived. At that time, the study is deleted from Ascribe. You can see a list of studies that have been shipped to your FTP site under Supervisor/Shipped Studies.</td></tr></tbody></table>
