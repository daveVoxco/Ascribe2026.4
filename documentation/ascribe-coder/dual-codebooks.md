# Dual Codebooks

Dual Codebooks page displays two fully functional codebook editors; users can perform any codebook editing operations. There is no functional difference between the two sides of the page. Users can copy/paste freely from one side to the other.

Dual Codebooks has two codebook panes, one on the left and right side of the screen. When you first open Dual Codebooks, the left Codebook Pane will be blank - click the Left Codebook icon (<img src="https://static.goascribe.com/Help/left_codebook_icon.gif" alt="" data-size="line">) to search for and display codebooks. The Right Codebook pane will be the codebook for the question you are currently working in; to change that codebook, click the Right Codebook icon (<img src="https://static.goascribe.com/Help/right_codebook_icon.gif" alt="" data-size="line">) to search for a different codebook.

Each pane has a toolbar that is identical to the codebook toolbar in Ascribe Coder; see [Codebook Pane Toolbar](./#minitocbookmark6) for more information. &#x20;

## Copying Codes from Another Codeframe <a href="#minitocbookmark3" id="minitocbookmark3"></a>

Copy and paste codes as desired between the codeframes. (Note: The codebooks displayed are not copies of the codebook but the actual codebook. Changes made to the codeframes in Dual Codebooks will be made to every question containing the codebook.)

Users have the ability to copy entire codebooks, nets, subnets, and individual codes. If you wish to copy multiple codes, select the codes, right-click then choose Copy Selected Codes and paste as desired.
