# Move a Group of Codes

You can only move codes when the codebook is **unlocked**. Moving a group of codes or a large number of codes may be easier with the codebook right-click menu option of Move Selection.

First, select the code/codes/net/nets that you want to move.

Then, right-click the code or net where you want to move the selection and click Move Selection. Choose either **into** (if you have selected a net) or **below** the currently selected location.

In the example below, Codes 211, 212, and 213 are selected. We right-clicked Miscellaneous Efficacy and are using Move Selection>Into to move the selected codes into the net.
