# Check Spelling

The Microsoft Word-based spell checking mechanism has been replaced with a new system based on the well-regarded Hunspell system, which is used in software such as Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox and OpenOffice.org. There is initial support for 50 dictionaries, including English (US) + Medical dictionary and English (British) + Medical dictionary. Users can also add custom words to their own user and account dictionaries.

At this time, the new spell check is available on the Edit Responses, Edit Respondent, Translation/Transcription pages, and the Taxonomies page in Inspector, as well as in Ascribe Coder.

To use the spell checker in Ascribe Coder, right-click the codebook node and select Check Spelling. In the Translation/Transcription pages, click the Check Spelling button at the top of the screen after you have typed a translation or transcription. In Edit Responses and Edit Respondent, enable the spell checker by selecting the check spelling box at the top of the window. Then right-click a response and select Spell Check All.

When a misspelling is found, the word is displayed in the dialog. Right-click it to see suggested corrections. Click the misspelling to put the cursor on the word in the response or in the codebook.

Here are the right-click menu options:
