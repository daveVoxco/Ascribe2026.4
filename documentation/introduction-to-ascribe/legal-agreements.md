# Legal Agreements

Ascribe is licensed software, subject to license agreements. These agreements are available on the website. When you log in for the first time, you are asked to review and accept the agreements. Both the End User Agreement and Ascribe Services Agreement can be viewed on the Ascribe website under the Agreements link. The date you accepted these agreements displays on the page.
