# Log In

To log in to Ascribe, enter the URL below in your browser address box:

**https://app.goascribe.us/ascribe/Logon.aspx**

Remember to use the s (http<mark style="color:red;">**s**</mark>) in the address prefix so your traffic will be encrypted. Ascribe uses 128 bit, public key encryption from a subsidiary of Verisign called Thawte.

The login page displays.

Enter the account name and your username. [Single sign-on users](single-sign-on-overview.md) should enter their email address.

Click Continue and enter your password.

## Forgot Password

If you forgot your password or need to reset it, click the Get a New Password link on the Log In page.

On the next screen, click the Send Me a One Time Password link. Check your email for a message from Ascribe which has the one time password. Copy/paste the password into the dialog.

Click Log In with One Time Password.

You are prompted to enter a new password.

Passwords must be at least 8 characters and must contain at least one uppercase letter, at least one lowercase letter, at least one number, a special character. It cannot contain spaces.
